JOB TITLE: Senior AI / Machine Learning Engineer - Core Intelligence
COMPANY: Google
LOCATION: Mountain View, CA / San Francisco, CA / Remote

ABOUT GOOGLE AI:
At Google, our mission is to organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful. Google Research and Core AI teams drive the frontier of artificial intelligence, advancing Gemini models, next-generation agent systems, scalable RAG architectures, and high-performance machine learning infrastructure powering billions of users daily.

ROLE OVERVIEW:
We are looking for an exceptional Senior AI Engineer to join the Core Intelligence team. In this role, you will design, train, fine-tune, and deploy state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs) and intelligent agent systems. You will collaborate closely with research scientists, infrastructure engineers, and product teams to deliver transformative AI capabilities across Google products.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Architect, implement, and optimize scalable LLM training, fine-tuning (LoRA/QLoRA), and inference pipelines using PyTorch, JAX, or TensorFlow.
- Design high-throughput RAG systems and hybrid dense/sparse vector retrieval algorithms for large-scale knowledge bases.
- Build production agentic workflows and tool-calling framework prototypes with strict latency, safety, and reliability guarantees.
- Conduct quantitative benchmark evaluations (accuracy, latency, semantic quality) to continuously drive model performance improvements.
- Quantize and compress large models for efficient deployment using TensorRT, ONNX, and specialized hardware accelerators (TPU/GPU).
- Lead technical design discussions, mentor junior engineers, and drive best practices in code quality, CI/CD, and system monitoring.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- M.S. or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, or equivalent quantitative discipline.
- 5+ years of hands-on experience building, fine-tuning, and deploying production machine learning models and LLMs.
- Expertise in Python, C++, and deep learning frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX, Hugging Face Transformers).
- Demonstrated experience with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), vector databases (ChromaDB, FAISS), and embedding search.
- Deep understanding of model optimization techniques: quantization (INT8/4-bit), TensorRT, ONNX, and distributed computing.
- Strong software engineering fundamentals: REST APIs (FastAPI/Flask), Docker, Kubernetes, microservices, system design, and CI/CD.
- Proven track record of technical leadership, cross-functional collaboration, and delivering high-impact AI solutions.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- First-author or co-author publications at top-tier machine learning conferences (NeurIPS, ACL, ICML, ICLR, CVPR).
- Contributions to major open-source AI repositories (Transformers, LangChain, LlamaIndex, vLLM).
- Experience with agentic frameworks, multi-agent orchestrations, and LLM evaluation benchmarks.
- Experience with cloud platforms (GCP, AWS) and large-scale TPU/GPU cluster management.
