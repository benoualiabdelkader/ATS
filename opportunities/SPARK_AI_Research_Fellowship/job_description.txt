**Opportunity Title:**
SPARK AI Research Fellowship (Remote)

**Organization:**
SPARK AI Research

**Opportunity Type:**
Remote AI Research Fellowship

**Field:**
Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning / AI Research

**Description:**
The SPARK AI Research Fellowship is a remote research opportunity designed for students and early-career individuals interested in advancing their knowledge and experience in Artificial Intelligence.

Fellows participate in AI-focused research activities, collaborate with mentors and other participants, explore emerging AI technologies, and contribute to projects related to machine learning, natural language processing, data science, and applied artificial intelligence.

The fellowship aims to provide participants with practical research exposure, mentorship, and opportunities to develop technical and research skills while working in an international AI community.

**Main Activities:**

* Participate in AI research projects under mentorship.
* Explore current topics in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
* Conduct literature reviews and analyze research papers.
* Develop experiments, prototypes, or AI-based solutions.
* Collaborate remotely with researchers and other fellows.
* Improve research communication and technical skills.

**Required Skills / Background:**

* Interest in Artificial Intelligence and emerging technologies.
* Basic knowledge of programming (especially Python is beneficial).
* Understanding of machine learning concepts is an advantage.
* Ability to learn independently and work remotely.
* Good written and verbal communication skills.

**Preferred Background:**

* Computer Science students.
* Artificial Intelligence students.
* Data Science / Machine Learning enthusiasts.
* Students interested in research careers.

**Mode:**
Remote

**Eligibility:**
Open to students and early-career candidates interested in AI research.

**Availability:**
Candidate available from July 2027.

**Candidate Profile Match:**
Master’s student in Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity with experience in AI projects, NLP, software development, and educational AI systems.

**Application Status:**
Contacting organization for future fellowship opportunities and possible funding/scholarship availability.
