--- PROJECT: EchoBreaker ---
TYPE: Hackathon Project (UNESCO Youth Hackathon 2026)
ROLE: Developer & Designer
PERIOD: 2026

--- WHAT THE PROJECT DOES ---
EchoBreaker is an innovative educational platform merging the digital and physical worlds (Phygital) aimed at promoting algorithmic literacy among youth. The project focuses on teaching users how recommendation algorithms work on social media platforms, and how to break out of "Echo Chambers" to regain control over the digital content directed at them.

--- KEY CONTRIBUTIONS ---
- Developed an "Algorithm Simulator": An interactive web application allowing users to visualize the impact of their interactions on content narrowing and algorithmic targeting in real-time.
- Designed the "Echo Escape" experience: A hybrid educational game applying an innovative "4A" framework to enhance critical thinking and the understanding of information amplification and flow mechanisms.
- Prepared the "Agency Toolkit": A practical toolkit empowering users to modify and control their personal account algorithms, helping them become ambassadors of digital awareness in their communities.

--- IMPACT & TECHNOLOGIES ---
- Technologies Used: HTML5, CSS, JavaScript (Frontend), Firebase (Backend & Cloud), UI/UX Design and Interactive Algorithm Simulation.
- Enhances Media and Information Literacy (MIL) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) skills.
- Provides radical solutions to address algorithmic bias, fully aligned with UNESCO's Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
